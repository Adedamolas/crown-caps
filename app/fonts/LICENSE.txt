Chosen because it is metrically compatible with Times New Roman, which is what
Rehoboth's original poster is set in. Compared side by side against the poster,
Tinos matches its width, stroke contrast and serif treatment; the previous
choice (Instrument Serif) was visibly narrower and higher-contrast.

Bundled here (rather than loaded via next/font) because Open Graph images are
rendered by Satori, which needs the raw font binary at render time. The web
pages still load this face through next/font.
